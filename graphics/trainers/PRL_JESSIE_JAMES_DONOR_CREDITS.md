# Jessie and James sprite donor credits

The Jessie and James battle portraits and walking frames in this folder were extracted from the owner-approved `PRL_Team_Rocket_COMPLETE_Donor_Bank_2026-10-09.zip`, directory `01_Trio_Approved_Donor_Sprites`. The selected fronts are the classic white-uniform variants; the walking assets are the original nine-frame 16×32 GBA 4bpp sets with their original RGB555 palettes.

The archive identifies the donor ROM as **Pokémon Team Rocket Jessie & James** (FireRed, BPRE), by **MrNightology**, source ROM SHA-256 `03b53a3594d7f67afe3c4b6de7127027d8ae4ac64961b59a51fe1cbd0578fe53`. PRL's archive notes say to verify individual graphical credits and reuse terms before public distribution. These are donor graphics only; no donor story, maps, code, or non-approved outfit sprites are imported.
