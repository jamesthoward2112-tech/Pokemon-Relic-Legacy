# PRL Step 10 map/event gates

These mechanics are intentionally not encoded as ordinary anywhere evolutions:
- Sceptile + Ancient Stone Lv45+ -> Sceptitan: Route 120 Forest Altar only.
- Blaziken + Ancient Stone Lv45+ -> Solaziken: Route 120 Sun Altar only.
- Swampert + Ancient Stone Lv45+ -> Swamplith: Route 120 Tide Altar only.
- Exact returning special Kanto Shiny Relicanth -> Coelossus: Sealed Chamber First Current after all ten guardians.
- nuMew, Astrachi, Alphoracle and Relic Ho-Oh are static scripted encounters.

Do not add fallback species-table evolutions that bypass these story gates.
