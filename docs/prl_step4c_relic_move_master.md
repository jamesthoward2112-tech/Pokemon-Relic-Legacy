Their positions need manual in-game walkability/visual validation. **Ecruteak City does not exist in this source checkout**, so no Johto NPC is claimed or placed. Place it on imported Johto map when available, ideally near the approved Rock Shop. Current three-badge/five-badge flags are chapter-independent and require cross-region reset scrutiny.
- **Engine whitelist:** `CanLearnTeachableMove` intercepts all eight first-batch moves and denies any custom/noncustom species lacking the locked TM/TUTOR/EXCLUSIVE route. LEVELUP-only routes never imply TM/TUTOR learnability, and existing Relic signatures, abilities, donor learnsets and Skulberus innates remain unchanged.
